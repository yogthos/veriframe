You have an engine-confirmed result. That makes this the moment to widen, not
only to deepen.

Name the two or three genuinely different ways to build on what you just
proved, and call `branch_theses` with them. The first thesis commits you and
the rest become sibling branches exploring in parallel, each with its own
engines and its own history, each seeing what the others confirm.

What counts as different: a different technique on the same claim, a
generalisation versus a sharper special case, an attack on the obstruction you
just found versus an attempt to route around it. What does not count: three
restatements of the plan you already have. If every route you can name is the
same route, say so by continuing rather than forking — but say it by doing the
work, not by declining out of caution.

Forking costs another engine process and another model call per turn. A
confirmed result is what pays for it.
